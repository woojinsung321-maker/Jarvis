      Privacy

      The microphone is only used after you tap the mic button, and stops when you tap it again, send, lock, or leave the app. JARVIS does not record, save or upload audio.

      Speech-to-text is done by your browser, not by JARVIS. In Chrome on Android the browser sends the audio to Google's speech service to transcribe it. If that is not acceptable, type instead.
